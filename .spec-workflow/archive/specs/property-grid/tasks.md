# Tasks Document

> **A small backlog, and deliberately so.** The property grid is already built and merged (`2b70a8e`); the requirements and design were written after it to fix the contract before its third and fourth consumers ([`azure-pipeline-diagram`](../azure-pipeline-diagram/requirements.md) Requirement 13, [`ansible-structure-diagram`](../ansible-structure-diagram/requirements.md) Requirement 10). So this document is **not** a rebuild. It closes the two gaps the design found between the approved requirements and the shipped code, adds the guards that keep the load-bearing rules from regressing quietly, and ends with a pass on the real app.
>
> **Order.** Tasks 1–2 are the backend gap and its tests; 3–4 the client gap and its tests; 5 the regression guards for rules that are honoured today but pinned by nothing; 6 the manual pass. Tasks 1–2 and 3–4 are independent of each other and may land in either order.
