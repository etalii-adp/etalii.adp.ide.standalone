## An amendment this task owes the document

**Not carded**, on the Scrum master's instruction, because Architect 2 owns the document and the board has moved off specification work. Recorded verbatim so whoever cards it is copying rather than re-deriving.

Task 3's guard clause reads:

> **Guard: mounted, over every registered canvas** — push a refusal and fail if no message appears, if more than one surface shows it, or if a module declares one.

**It needs a fourth condition: the one surface that appears is the library's.** *"More than one surface shows it"* catches a module that **adds** to the library's surface. It does not catch one that **replaces** it - one surface appears, the count is right, and it is the wrong surface. Three modules are exactly that case: `wardley-rejection`, `helm-canvas-rejection` and `dotnet-dependency-canvas-rejection` do not compose `canvas-rejection` at all, where eleven others do. **A blind spot distributed exactly over the three modules furthest from what the task wants is the worst shape available.**

Also owed: task 3's prose names *"the three module loading/unavailable blocks"*, and the rule beside it - *no module declares a rejection or status element* - covers **four** modules plus a shell surface. `ShaclCanvas.tsx:324` renders a `shacl-loading` paragraph with no unavailable counterpart, and `c4` has both blocks. **The rule and the prose disagree and the rule is the one to implement.**
