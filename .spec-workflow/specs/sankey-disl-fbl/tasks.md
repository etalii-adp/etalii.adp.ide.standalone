# Tasks Document

One Developer owns this specification, per CLAUDE.md's one-owner-per-specification rule, and works it in one worktree (`.claude/worktrees/skv`, branch `features/sankey-disl-fbl`). **The order is the design's eleven steps and it is load-bearing**: the transcript is frozen before anything changes (task 1), derivation is switched before reading (tasks 6 and 7, then task 10), and reading before writing (task 11), so that a difference in the transcript can be attributed to one change. The design's step 4 is two tasks here, the library work (task 4) and the bundling (task 5), because together they are too large to review as one pull request; every other task is one design step.

**Every task is one pull request into `develop` and leaves the four gates green.** A task whose transcript differs in a way this document does not name is not delivered.

**Task 3 is done in etalii-adp/etalii.adp**, through that repository's own pull request. Task 5 cannot start before it is merged there. Tasks 10 and 12 each end with a second, small pull request in etalii.adp (the binding offered there, and the companion completed), named in the task.

**Tasks 9, 10, 11 and 12 wait, and nobody starts them early.**

- **Task 9 waits on a language change in etalii-adp/etalii.adp**: FBL gaining a slot that reads a YAML scalar as written (gap 1) and a slot whose value will not read without losing its entry (gap 2). That change is etalii.adp's own specification. Until it is merged there with its conformance fixtures, task 9 has nothing to vendor and is not started.
- **Gap 1 is shared with `timeline-disl-fbl`** and is delivered to `EtAlii.Adp.Specification.Fbl` once. Whichever of the two specifications reaches its reading step first vendors the fixture and adds the construct; the other uses it. Before starting task 9 its owner asks the Scrum master whether the timeline's Developer has already delivered it, and task 9 then shrinks to gap 2.
- **Tasks 10, 11 and 12 wait on task 9**, each on the one before it. Until then the module keeps `SankeyParser` and `SankeyWriter`, and Requirements 3.4, 4, 5, 6, 7.2 and 9.3 stay open.
- **Tasks 1 to 8 do not wait** and nothing in them is thrown away by the wait. Task 8 needs only the bundled file and may land at any time after task 5.

**This document is written against the recommended option of each amendment the design proposes; a ruling that differs is a small amendment to the tasks named.** The first amendment (strike `cel.bind` from finding A11) touches tasks 1 and 4: ruled otherwise, task 1 does not strike it and task 4 records in its pull request that nothing was added for it. The second amendment (Requirement 9.3 waits with Requirement 4) touches tasks 4 and 9: ruled otherwise, the claim of 9.3 moves from task 9 to task 4 as a change that adds nothing until the language moves.
