# Tasks Document

> **Order.** Tasks 1–6 are the backend engine (the seam, the traversal, the store, the startup pass) and are **independently mergeable**: after task 6 the backend finds and remembers a project's problems, with nothing yet able to see them. Tasks 7–10 add the contract and the delivery, 11–14 the client, 15 the manual pass. Task 7 changes `context.proto`, so 7–14 land in one continuous pass — the client cannot render a `problems` message before the contract carries one.
>
> **The one line that matters.** Task 12 makes the panel report itself as the selection on focus. That single line is what puts **Validate all** in the ribbon and in the panel's right-click menu; nothing in `RibbonContextualGroups` or `ContextMenu` is touched by this spec (design *ErrorsWarningsPanel*, Requirement 7.4).
