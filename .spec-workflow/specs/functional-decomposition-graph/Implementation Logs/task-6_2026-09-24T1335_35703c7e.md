# Implementation Log: Task 6

**Summary:** Committed as 88b3bc1f, landed on develop in d8adc1e5.

outlineEdgePoint is the third reader of the one outline, after the drawing and the text region: the ray from the shape's centre towards the other end, met against the outline itself. The attachment resolver asks for it and falls back to edgePointOf on a null, so box, pill and everything else without an outline keep exactly today's geometry. It ASKS rather than branching on shape names - a list at the call site would be a second copy of OUTLINED_SHAPES to keep in step by hand, and a null from the function that owns the outlines cannot drift from them. The nearest crossing is taken, correct for any outline star-shaped about its centre, which all six are; that word is said in the function so a concave shape added later needs it said again rather than silently inheriting it.

TASK 6 MOVES NOTHING IN THE SHIPPING PRODUCT. diamond, hexagon and parallelogram are declared by no module in the tree, and the shapes modules actually use are box, pill, frame, span, symbol, none and timeline's moment. So the suite's green is evidence that NOTHING ASSERTED THE OLD ATTACHMENT POINTS, not evidence that the new ones look right - and the new guard is the only thing exercising them until the module half lands. A reader who finds a green suite next to "connectors meet the outline" will conclude it was verified in the product, and it was not.

SEEN TO FAIL against today's bounding-box point, with the failure naming the coordinate: "-80,12 is not on or inside the trapezoid". At y 12 the trapezoid's own left boundary is at -62, so the arrowhead was drawn 18 units beside the shape it points at. The diamond case failed the same way.

THE GUARD IS WRITTEN AGAINST isInsideOutline, NOT AGAINST THE ARITHMETIC. A test that recomputed the crossing would agree with the implementation however wrong both were. "On or inside the outline, and half a unit further along the ray is outside it" is a statement about the drawn shape that holds whatever the arithmetic looks like, and it is what pins the point to the edge rather than merely to the interior. The point is read off the RENDERED PATH rather than from the resolver, because the drawn line is what a reader sees and the canvas has exactly one attachment resolver behind it (Requirement 3.4).

THE ANGLE IS CHOSEN, AND THE CHOICE IS THE TEST: a steeper approach leaves through the trapezoid's BOTTOM edge, where the outline and the bounding box agree and the whole question is invisible.

WHICH CHECKS ARE WHAT: the trapezoid and the diamond/superellipse loop are the detectors. The box case is a no-regression companion that passes in both worlds, kept because giving box an outline would move every rectangle in every diagram in the product and nothing else would say so. The loop exists because a guard naming one shape cannot tell a general implementation from a special case for trapezoids.

THE DELETED CONNECTIONS COMPONENTS ARE CASE ONE, NOT CASE TWO. canvas/connections/{bezier,fixed-bezier,interactive-bezier,straight} were removed on develop by Architect 4 with the user's approval, nothing having drawn them since 2026-09-06. This requirement is met against the current location - the attachment resolver in DiagramCanvas.tsx - and nothing in the approved design depends on those components existing.
