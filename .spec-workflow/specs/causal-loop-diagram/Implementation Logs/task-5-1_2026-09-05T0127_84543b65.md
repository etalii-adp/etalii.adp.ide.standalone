# Implementation Log: Task 5.1

**Summary:** Two authored examples, replicated into the showcase, plus a byte-shape guard that came out of a defect found while writing them. reference/ carries all five hard cases together — a reinforcing loop, a balancing loop, a delayed link, a variable in three loops, and R3, which claims reinforcing where three negative links make it balancing. on-call/ is the readable one: why an on-call rotation gets worse on its own. A fresh licence search found a fifth candidate, bear96/System-Dynamics-Bot, the only one carrying an actual corpus of causal loop diagrams rather than a tool; it is CC BY-NC-4.0, so it joins the four recorded rejections. Both examples are authored for this repository, labelled as authored, attributed to nobody. TWO DEFECTS WERE FOUND BY WRITING THESE, one of them in the product's own favour: the validator caught the author mislabelling on-call's third loop B3, and it was right on the arithmetic AND on the systems thinking — two negative links is an even count, so "too busy to stop being busy" is reinforcing, not a starved balancing loop. The other was a raw NUL byte in CausalLoopValidator.cs that made the file binary to git and grep; it now has a guard.
