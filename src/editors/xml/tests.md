# XML editor: browser checks

What only a running application shows, from the End-to-End section of the [design](https://github.com/etalii-adp/etalii.adp.ide.standalone/blob/bd7e2ea95b8e4195bf6f8d97f7aede9ab4dc3ad8/.spec-workflow/specs/xml-editor/design.md). Run them against a local build, signed in with the checked-in `admin`/`changeme` placeholder, with `src/examples/` open as the project. Every check is done in the light theme and again in the dark theme. The results below were taken in a cloud session, in headless Chromium driven by Playwright, the theme switched by the browser's colour-scheme preference.

| # | Check | Steps | Expected | Result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Colour in both themes | Open `editors/xml/order.xml`. Switch the theme. | Element names, attribute names and values, the comment, the declaration and the `p:` prefixes each in their own colour; the colours follow the switch without reopening the tab. | Passed (2026-10-13), light and dark. Each kind in its own colour, the `p:` prefix apart from its local name (light rgb(130,80,223) beside rgb(179,21,106), dark rgb(210,168,255) beside rgb(255,123,195)); the colours followed the switch without reopening; the status line read "Schema: urn:example:orders, urn:example:parties". |
| 2 | A tag closing as it is typed, and one undo | In `notes.xml`, inside `<notes>`, type `<note>`. Press Ctrl+Z. Type `<note>text</`. | `</note>` appears after the caret as `>` is typed, and one undo takes it away; after `</` the name of the open element is completed. | Passed (2026-10-13), light and dark. `<note>` became `<note></note>` and one Ctrl+Z took `</note>` away; `text</` became `text</note>`, and one undo took `note>` away. |
| 3 | A broken XSD constraint's three carriers | Open `library-broken.xml`. | The missing `isbn`, the missing `year`, `soon` as a year and the `publisher` element are each marked where they are, with an underline, a gutter mark and the sentence on hover and on F8; the status line counts the errors and names `library.xsd`. | Passed (2026-10-13), light and dark. `book`, `book`, `soon` and `publisher` underlined with four gutter marks; hover and F8 gave "The required attribute 'isbn' is missing." with its source; the status line read "Saved, 4 errors, Schema: library.xsd". |
| 4 | Completion from the keyboard alone | In `library.xml`, after the last `</book>`, type `<`, choose `book` with the arrow keys and Enter, then Tab; inside the start tag press Ctrl+Space and choose `format`; inside its quotes press Ctrl+Space. | `book` is inserted with `isbn=""` and the caret in it, then between the tags; `format` is offered with `isbn` already written left out; `hardback`, `paperback` and `ebook` are offered, and the schema's documentation is beside each item that has one. | Passed (2026-10-13), light and dark. `<` offered `book` with "One book on the shelf." beside it; Enter gave `<book isbn=""></book>` with the caret in the quotes, Tab moved it between the tags; Ctrl+Space in the start tag offered `format` alone; in its quotes `ebook`, `hardback` and `paperback`. `format` and its values have no documentation in `library.xsd`, so none is shown for them. |
| 5 | Editing the XSD in a second tab | Open `library.xml` and `library.xsd`. In `library.xsd` make `year` optional and save. | `library.xml` is checked again with no edit of its own, and a document that lacked a year is no longer marked for it. | Passed (2026-10-13), light and dark. With a book's `year` removed and saved, `lent` was marked where `year` was expected; after `year` was made optional in `library.xsd` and saved, `library.xml` showed no finding and "Saved, Schema: library.xsd". Going back to the first tab opens its editor afresh, so the same was done with `library.xml` left open and `library.xsd` changed on disk: the same editor dropped the mark with no edit of its own. |
| 6 | The backend stopped | With `library.xml` open, stop the backend. Type in the document. | Colour, the tag at the caret's partner and folding stay; the status line says "Checking is unavailable." once. | Passed (2026-10-13), light and dark. After the backend process was killed and the document typed in three times, the status line said "Checking is unavailable." once; the same six colours stayed, the caret in `<title>` marked both `title` tags, and folding a `book` hid its five lines. |
